Privacy Policy

MealBridge · Flankin Saint · Last updated: August 17, 2026

Summary: MealBridge collects minimal data. We use Google Sign-In for accounts, send your fridge photos to Google Gemini AI for analysis (photos are not stored by us), and save your meal plans to the cloud only when you choose to sync. We never sell your data.

1. Who We Are

MealBridge is a meal planning application developed by Flankin Saint. The app helps you scan your fridge and generate personalized meal plans using artificial intelligence.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: mucyoherve2023@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

Account Information

  • Your Google account name and email address — collected when you sign in with Google
  • A unique user ID assigned by Firebase Authentication
  • Your Google profile photo (displayed in the app only)

Meal Plan Data

  • Meal plans you generate — stored locally on your device by default
  • Meal plan history — uploaded to our cloud database ONLY when you manually tap "Sync my data" in your profile
  • Your meal preferences (liked and disliked meals) — stored locally on your device only, never uploaded

Subscription Information

  • Your subscription status (active, trial, expired) — managed by RevenueCat
  • Purchase history — managed by Google Play and RevenueCat
  • We never see or store your payment card details

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Your location
  • Your contacts
  • Your health or medical data
  • Any data from children under 13
  • Your camera photos (see AI section below)

3. Artificial Intelligence Disclosure Important

MealBridge uses Bridge AI, powered by the Google Gemini API, to analyze photos you take and generate meal plans. This section explains exactly how your data is handled during AI processing.

What happens when you take a fridge photo:

  • The photo is sent to Google Gemini API for food identification
  • Gemini returns a list of identified food items
  • The photo is NOT stored by MealBridge on any server
  • The photo is NOT stored permanently by Google Gemini beyond the processing request
  • The photo is discarded from your device memory after analysis

What happens when you generate a meal plan:

  • Your confirmed ingredient list is sent to Google Gemini API
  • Your cuisine preference and people count are included
  • Gemini returns a meal plan
  • This data is NOT stored by MealBridge on any server beyond the generated meal plan itself

By using MealBridge you acknowledge that photos and ingredient data are processed by Google Gemini API and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy .

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To generate personalized meal plans using AI
  • To sync your meal plan history across devices when you request it
  • To manage your subscription and access to premium features
  • To remember your meal preferences for better future suggestions
  • To send you meal reminders and plan expiry notifications (only if you grant permission)

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your data to any third party. Ever.

5. Third Party Services

MealBridge uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication + Firestore) — account management and optional cloud backup. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini API — AI-powered food analysis and meal plan generation. Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — subscription management and purchase verification. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — payment processing. MealBridge never sees your payment card details. Google Privacy Policy

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Local data (preferences, cached meal plans) is stored on your device using Android Room database and SharedPreferences
  • Cloud data (meal plan history when synced) is stored on Google Firebase Firestore servers with encryption in transit and at rest
  • Account data is managed by Google Firebase Authentication with industry-standard security
  • We do not operate our own servers. All cloud infrastructure is managed by Google Firebase

7. Data Retention

  • Local data remains on your device until you delete the app or delete your account
  • Cloud data remains until you delete your account
  • Photos taken for fridge scanning are not retained — they are processed and discarded immediately

8. Your Rights and Account Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data (contact us at the email below)

To delete your account:

  1. Open MealBridge
  2. Tap your profile icon (top right of home screen)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete my account"
  4. Confirm deletion

Account deletion permanently removes your Google account connection, all synced meal plan history, and your MealBridge profile. Local data on your device is also cleared. This action cannot be undone.

If you have an active subscription, please cancel it in Google Play settings before deleting your account. Account deletion does not automatically cancel your Google Play subscription.

9. Children's Privacy

MealBridge is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at mucyoherve2023@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

10. Push Notifications

MealBridge may send you local push notifications including:

  • Daily meal reminders (based on your current plan)
  • Meal plan expiry alerts

You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings. We do not send marketing or promotional push notifications without your explicit consent.
